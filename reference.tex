\begin{minipage}[t]{0.46\textwidth}
    \cvsect{Summary}
	\vspace{-6pt}
 
    %Dummy text
	I am a Robotics AI researcher developing scalable frameworks for multimodal action learning, with the goal of enabling human-level manipulation. My research focuses on collecting large-scale multimodal datasets—including vision, force, and tactile inputs—from both simulation and real-world environments, and training action models that generalize to contact-rich manipulation tasks. Ultimately, I aim to deploy these models on dual-arm robotic platforms to achieve human-like bimanual manipulation. \\
